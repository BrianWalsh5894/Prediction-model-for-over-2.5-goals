# EV bucket test: is a bigger claimed edge real, or model error?

DIAGNOSTIC ONLY. Backtest seasons 2024/25 + 2025/26 (already spent). Flat 1 unit, better side, EV >= 3% after 2% commission, at the Betfair early price and at the Betfair close. Nothing here changes the live 3% rule.

- `value_at_close`: EV of the bet if the de-margined Betfair close is the true probability (much less noisy than ROI; positive = beat the closing line).
- `model_gap_pp`: model probability of the side minus Betfair close probability.
- `mkt_move_pp`: close minus early probability of the side; positive = market moved towards the model (only meaningful at the early price).
- `small` = fewer than 50 bets - too small to read.

Matches per group: England upper (PL+Champ) 1864, England lower (L1+L2) 2208, eu_upper (top 4 Europe) 2744, eu_second (Europe 2nd div) 2907, conference (National League) 1104
